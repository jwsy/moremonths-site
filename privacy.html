MoreMonths Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

MoreMonths is a calendar widget made by an independent developer. It is built to collect as little as possible.

What MoreMonths does not collect

  • No account, name, email address, or contact details
  • No calendar events, contacts, photos, or location
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking across apps or websites

The widget draws its calendar entirely on your device.

Purchases

Supporting MoreMonths (the yearly or lifetime option) is handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details.

To verify purchases and restore them across your devices, MoreMonths uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, your purchase history for MoreMonths, and basic device information such as the OS version and storefront country. This data is used only to confirm what you have purchased and is not used for advertising.

Data stored on your device

MoreMonths stores the start date of your free trial in your device's Keychain and shares your support status with its widget through an App Group on the same device. This information never leaves your device except as described above.

Children

MoreMonths does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
